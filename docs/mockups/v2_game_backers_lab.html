CAWho Is On This Game Labseven directions for the game page, phone first, same nine cappers in every one
Every variant shows the same fictional game and the same nine cappers, sorted by NFL money. Tap a pill to switch. Names in this lab are from the Sept 3 export, records and picks are invented for the mock. Rules every variant holds: no scores, no strength meters, counts are counts, both sides equal, Grading Note at the bottom.
Ledger. One flat ledger of every capper, filtered by side with a no-JS radio strip and a six-slot count grid above it.
Ledger keeps one flat list of all nine cappers, sorted by NFL money descending, with a radio-driven side filter (All / DAL / CHI / Over / Under) and a six-slot count grid so every slot reads as a slot even when the list is filtered. Each row is a full-width anchor with a chevron, which is the tap affordance and needs no JS. Both sides carry identical row treatment, and counts are plain numbers with no meter. This revision resolves the five layout risks without touching the visual idea: (1) the Covers price string now breaks at a deliberate point ("no price," / "graded at -110") with nowrap on each line, so it is two tidy right-aligned lines under the chip instead of an uncontrolled wrap, and the break collapses back to one line at 560px+ where the column is wider; (2) the filter tabs drop to 12px with 2px side padding, 3px gaps and overflow hidden, which leaves roughly 20px of slack per tab at 362px and clips inside the tab under larger OS text instead of pushing the strip; (3) the middle column gains about 18px (disc 34 to 32, row gap 10 to 8, right padding 10 to 8, chip padding 9 to 8, metric gap 8 to 7) so "22-11 +$188 +14.2% ROI" sits on one line with room to spare; (4) the empty state is now wired with :has(), so a filter whose side has no rows hides the list and shows "No cappers on this side" (harmless no-op on the current fixture, and browsers without :has simply ignore those two rules); (5) role=tablist is replaced with role=group plus the same aria-label, which is the honest semantic for a set of labels driving hidden radios.
Free tier: Free tier renders the identical layout with the same nine rows, the same filter strip, the same six-slot counts and the same money/record/ROI cells. Only the .v1-name cell is redacted per row (a muted placeholder bar the same height as the name, initials disc still shown so the row keeps its shape), and the anchor href points at the subscribe flow instead of the profile. One subscribe prompt sits directly under the list as a single line ("See who is on this game") styled like .v1-empty, never a blur wall or overlay. Counts, slot grid, humility line, unqualified line and Grading Note are always visible to everyone.
After the game: After the game is final the fragment freezes exactly as it was at kickoff (the count line already says "freezes at kickoff"). Each row's .v1-pchip gains a result state: a green ring plus a small W after the pick text for wins, a red ring plus L for losses, a muted ring plus P for pushes, while the price line stays as recorded so the reader can see the price the grade was struck at. The six-slot grid keeps its counts and appends the result letter next to each slot count. Money, record and ROI cells stay pre-game (they are the capper's NFL ledger going into the game, not updated in place). No sorting change, no side is promoted, the humility line and Grading Note stay verbatim.
Sides. Away on the left, home on the right, over and under below: the game reads like a scoreboard and every capper sits under the side they took.
Same idea as before: the card is a scoreboard. Away on the left, home on the right, over and under below, and each capper sits in the column of the side they took, so the six slots are read as two halves of one line rather than a list. Counts are plain chips, both halves mirror each other, and the cappers inside a column sort by NFL money then win percentage. Revision notes. The kickoff time, venue, and team records all come straight from the fixture (game.start, game.venue, game.awayRecord, game.homeRecord), so nothing here is invented; the .v2-when line stays. To keep the team record from reading like a capper record, it moved out of the column heads and now sits under the full team name in the matchup header ("Dallas Cowboys / 1-1"), well away from the capper rows and beside a full club name rather than a three-letter abbreviation. That also shortens the column heads to "DAL" plus the count chip, so the chip no longer risks wrapping. The Over/Under heads keep their juice (-110, from the fixture total line) under a separate .v2-px class so it is never styled as a record. The "no price, graded at -110" chip now sits on its own full-width line (.v2-pl) and breaks once, deliberately, after "no price," so it never splits mid-phrase in the narrow column. The stat line dropped to 11.5px with a tighter gap so record, money, and ROI fit on one line in the ~113px body on most rows. The mirrored chevron moved 2px inward with a 16px gutter so it clears the center divider with margin. Everything else is unchanged.
Free tier: Free tier keeps the whole scoreboard: matchup header, both column heads with their counts, the slot count lines, the total band, and every row's pick, price chip, record, money, ROI, source badge, and recorded time. Only the capper name cell is redacted, one per row, rendered as a muted blurred-text placeholder inside .v2-name (a short block in --surface2 with a --border ring, no real characters), and the initials disc shows a neutral dot instead of letters. Rows stay tappable but route to the subscribe sheet. One subscribe prompt, a single line in --accent beneath the count line, reads "Subscribe to see who these cappers are." The counts, the humility line, the unqualified line, and the grading note stay exactly as shown. No blur wall, no dimmed columns, no hidden slots.
After the game: After the game finalizes the layout does not move. The matchup header gets the final score under each team name where the record now sits (score in --text at 14px 700, record moved to muted small type next to it), and the .v2-when line reads "Final". Each row's pick line gets a small result chip after the price: W in --green, L in --red, Push in --muted, using the same .v2-chip shape. The slot count lines in the column heads do not change (counts are counts) and no side is marked as having "won". Money and ROI on each row stay the capper's NFL season figures, not the single-game result, so the numbers a reader tapped through pregame are the same numbers after. The total band shows the final total next to 44.5 in muted type. The humility line, the unqualified line, and the grading note are unchanged.
Market tabs. Three market panels (Win, Spread, Total), one open at a time, each split into two equal side columns; the other two fold to a one-line summary that still shows every slot and its count.
Same direction as before: one market in focus at a time, the other two folded to a one-line summary that still names every slot and its count, so all six slots are readable without opening anything. Revisions from review: (1) the team record spans are gone from every side header; (2) the (-110) juice is gone from the spread and total headers, leaving only the line values that are read straight off the capper picks (-1.5 / +1.5 / 44.5) and the ML prices that match the recorded capper prices (-125 / +105); (3) the "N picks" values remain and are W+L sums, which agrees with the fixture's nflPicks field where present (HomeRunHazard 9-6 = 15, Small sample); (4) every market panel is now a native <details> with a <summary> fold row, so folding and expanding works with zero JS, the chevron rotates on [open], the summary is keyboard focusable, and the Spread panel ships open. The tab pills are now anchors that jump to each panel by id (modern Chromium and WebKit auto-expand a closed details on fragment navigation), while the summary rows stay the primary expand affordance. The legend line moved above the panels so it covers all three markets. Sort inside each side stays NFL money descending. Both sides of each market get identical column weight.
Free tier: Free tier keeps the same skeleton: every side header, count, fold summary, record, money, ROI, pick, price chip and time stay visible. Only the capper name cell (and the initials disc) is redacted, one cell per row, replaced by a muted "Subscribe to see who" name and a blank disc. A single subscribe prompt sits once, directly under the tab strip, not per panel and not per row. No blur wall, no hidden counts, no locked panels: the details folds still open for free users.
After the game: After kickoff the count line changes to "9 cappers, 3 sources, frozen at kickoff" and the fold summaries are unchanged. Once graded, each row's pick line gains a result chip after the price chip (W in green, L in red, P muted) and each side header's count line reads e.g. "2 cappers, 2-0". Money, ROI and record on each row stay the pre-game NFL numbers so the row reflects what was known at capture time; the profile tab carries the updated ledger.
Odds board. A six-slot price board up top, every capper listed in full under the slot they took.
The board stays the visual idea: six price cells in a 2-wide grid (spread, moneyline, total), each showing the market price, an initials stack, and a plain count. What changed: (1) all six slot panels now render statically below the board, so every one of the 9 cappers appears as a full row (name, source badge, NFL record, money, ROI, picks, pick with line, price chip, recorded time) and every board cell is a real in-page jump link with no JS or :target dependency; (2) HomeRunHazard carries the Small sample chip and stevet81 carries the L5 snowflake chip because their rows now exist; (3) the game line (records, time, venue) is removed so the fragment carries only the board lines and capper data; (4) the permanent selected state on CHI +1.5 is gone, every cell and every panel share one neutral treatment, so both sides of every market carry equal weight; (5) row labels moved above each pair as a full-width caption, which widens each cell to about 164px at 362px and keeps 'Under 44.5' plus its price on one line, and the cell head and panel head both wrap instead of clipping. Rows inside each panel sort by NFL money descending. The hint copy now says the tap jumps to the list rather than reveals it.
Free tier: Free tier keeps the whole board visible: every cell still shows its price, initials stack count, and the plain capper count, and all six panels still render with their count lines. Inside each panel, every row keeps record, money, ROI, picks, pick, price chip, and recorded time, but the name is replaced with a muted 'Name hidden' label and the initials disc goes blank, one row at a time. A single subscribe prompt sits once, between the board and the first panel, in the same card style. No blur wall, nothing dimmed, tap targets on rows route to the subscribe prompt instead of the profile.
After the game: After kickoff the count line reads 'frozen at kickoff' and the tap hint drops. Each panel head gains a small result tag for that slot (Won in green, Lost in red, Push in muted) next to the count, and each board cell shows the same tag in place of the hover affordance so the board reads as a settled grid. Rows are unchanged apart from the result tag: the recorded times, prices, and records stay as captured, and nothing re-sorts. Both sides of every market still share the same neutral cell treatment; the result tag is the only difference.
Slot grid. All six slots as a two-column grid of equal cards, each side stacking its own cappers under a plain count.
The six slots are the organizing idea: a two-column grid where each market row (Moneyline, Spread, Total) holds the away/over slot on the left and the home/under slot on the right, so both sides always get the same width, the same header treatment and the same card style. Each slot header carries the pick label, the market price and a plain count ("2 cappers"), and the cappers stack underneath as tappable cards sorted by NFL money descending (ties by win percentage) with name, source badge, W-L, net money, ROI, price chip and recording time. Two content notes for the critic: (1) every value in the game header and the market rows is read from the shared `game` fixture, not invented: `awayRecord`/`homeRecord` give DAL 1-1 / CHI 1-1, `start` gives Sun 1:00 PM ET, `venue` gives Soldier Field, and `lines.ml` / `lines.spread` / `lines.total` give the row prices; the per-slot header prices are the matching halves of those same strings. (2) The unused `.wg-slot.is-empty` / `.wg-empty` rules were removed since no slot in this fixture is empty; the empty-slot treatment is documented in the graded-state notes instead of shipping as dead CSS. One small layout hardening from the risk list: the market-row price text now wraps instead of ellipsis-truncating, so "DAL -1.5 (-110) / CHI +1.5 (-110)" stays fully readable at 362px.
Free tier: Free tier keeps the whole grid, every slot header and every count exactly as rendered here. Inside each card the name cell (.wg-name) is replaced by a muted "Subscriber" placeholder of fixed width (one redacted cell per card, initials disc replaced by a neutral disc with no letters), while the source badge, record, money, ROI, price and recording time stay visible. One subscribe prompt sits directly under the grid, above the "4 more cappers" line, as a single-line text link in --accent. No blur wall, no dimming of the grid, no hidden slots.
After the game: Empty slot: a slot with nobody on it renders the same .wg-slot shell and header with a muted zero in .wg-n ("0 cappers") and, in place of the card stack, one muted line reading "No cappers on this side" styled like .wg-foot (10px, --muted, centered, 10px padding); the slot keeps its full column width so the opposite side never widens to fill the gap. After kickoff: the count line already says "freezes at kickoff", so post-kickoff the grid is unchanged except each card footer gains the graded result as a small chip after the recording time ("W", "L" or "Push" in --green / --red / --muted tint, same size as the Small sample chip) and the slot header count is unchanged. Void or postponed games show "Void" chips in --muted on every card and no color. Nothing re-sorts after grading; order stays NFL money descending as of kickoff.
Slips. Each capper is a torn betting slip: pick up top, their NFL ledger on the stub, filter pills by side.
A betting slip is the object every reader already understands, so each capper becomes one: name and source on the header, a dashed tear line with notches, the pick large on the body with their recorded price chip and when it was recorded, and their NFL ledger (record, money, ROI) on the stub. The six-slot grid up top is pure counts, all six slots present, with no slot prices so nothing is asserted beyond what was captured. Radio-driven filter pills (All, DAL, CHI, Over, Under) hide slips off the chosen side and dim the other slots, no JS. Cappers stay in one list sorted by NFL money descending so both sides keep equal weight. Revision: dropped invented game meta (records, kickoff, venue) down to DAL at CHI, removed the slot price column, removed the derived picks count (Small sample chip is the only sample-size signal), replaced per-card aria-label with a visually hidden "Open NFL profile" suffix so the full card text stays in the accessible name, filter bar now wraps and is no longer sticky, footer is three columns, and .v6 is position:relative so the hidden radios resolve against it.
Free tier: Free tier keeps every slip in place and every count visible (the six-slot grid, the count line, the filter pills). On each slip the name cell is replaced by a muted redacted bar of the same height (initials disc becomes a blank --surface2 disc), the source badge stays, and the pick, price, recorded time, record, money and ROI stay fully readable. One subscribe prompt sits between the slot grid and the list ("Subscribe to see who these cappers are"), never a blur wall, and the humility line, unqualified line and grading note render unchanged.
After the game: After the game finalizes, the count line reads "9 cappers, 3 sources, frozen at kickoff" and each slip's body gains a small result chip beside the price (W green, L red, Push muted, Void muted) sized like the streak chip. The pick and price never change since they were frozen at kickoff. Footer stats stay as the capper's NFL ledger at the time of capture, with a muted "at capture" note in the stat label so a later-updated record is never implied. Slot counts stay as recorded; no slot is marked as the winning side.
Split. Each market is a two-column face-off: one featured card per side, the rest tucked underneath, a count bar across the middle.
Two markets (Margin, Total), each rendered as a face-off: both sides get an identical column with an identical featured card (the side's highest NFL money capper) and a collapsible list of the rest, sorted by NFL money then win percentage. A count bar spans the middle so the two numbers sit at equal weight, never as a meter. Revisions: the Small sample chip now appears only on HomeRunHazard (15 picks); the game header, team records, and priced market lines were stripped because they are not implied by the picks (headers now read Moneyline and spread / Over and under 44.5, and the Over/Under blocks show only 44.5); featured stat labels carry the NFL qualifier; the root rule was moved to an inner .v7-wrap so every selector is .v7-prefixed; the price chip wraps instead of clipping; collapsed rows put money on line 2 so names get the full width; the featured stats grid became a wrapping flex row; and the split grid uses auto-fit with a 150px floor so it stacks to one column on very narrow screens.
