# CappingAlpha App Redesign: UI Research Synthesis

Sources: 10 research agents covering Action Network (6 verified screenshots from Jack), ESPN, theScore, DraftKings, FanDuel, Sleeper, Underdog, PrizePicks, Pikkit, Betstamp, plus two cross-app surveys (tab bars across 11 apps; onboarding across the category). Items flagged "unverified" in the underlying research are noted where they matter.

---

## A. Per-App Summaries and Most Stealable Ideas

**Action Network** is the closest comp: not a sportsbook, sells analytics on bets placed elsewhere, and its whole conversion engine is showing you the dashboard you will earn. Six-slot bar (HOME, TOP GAMES, PRO, ASK AI, PICKS, MY ACTION), no account tab (profile lives inside My Action behind gear and pencil icons), and Track-a-Bet is a green + FAB, not a tab slot. Most stealable: (1) the logged-out My Action empty state, a swipeable deck of fake-data analytics cards (record + equity sparkline, bet-type stacked bars, "+CLV Bets 567", Net Units area chart, Hot Streaks) over the headline "All your bets in one place." and one green "Get started!" button; (2) the persistent collapsible green banner above the tab bar while signed out ("Welcome - Start by creating an account"); (3) the three-layer bet history (period cards, per-sport cards with a star on your best sport, Favorite Bets distribution bars); (4) the bet row grammar (result badge overlapping the team logo, bold bet-notation title, score subline, colored P/L right).

**ESPN** proves 5 slots is enough forever (Home, Scores, Watch, Verts, More) by pushing everything else into sub-nav strips inside tabs and a persistent top-right gear for settings. Most stealable: the MY GAMES module (compact two-line score rows for your teams with a red live-state column and per-row bell), favorites-before-registration onboarding (account sold later as "sync everywhere"), badge-gating premium content (visible but locked tiles that open the plan sheet on tap), and attaching every notification ask to a concrete object (a bell on a game row), never a cold launch prompt. Notably, ESPN removed always-on odds blocks from Scores rows in early 2026; even they found ambient odds too noisy.

**theScore** runs 5 content slots (Favorites, Scores, News, Discover, Leagues) with account behind a top-left avatar, at 4M MAU scale. Most stealable: the Bet Mode toggle that transforms the Discover slot into a Bet section in place (opt-in, off by default, region-gated), the compliance handoff (build a betslip in-app, never place a wager in-app), per-favorite bell toggles that make push opt-in granular and contextual, and My Bets as Open / Settled segments. Its "one deeply scrolled river per tab" content model is its stated engagement driver.

**DraftKings** puts the money action in the center slot: the bet slip / My Bets hub is tab 3 of 5, account is demoted to a top avatar, and deposit is a permanent green + pinned top-right. Most stealable: the interruptible half-sheet bet slip (tap any odds cell, sheet pops, swipe down to keep browsing, count persists on the center tab), My Bets as a live surface (Open/Settled/Won/Lost filters, per-leg parlay tracking with live stats, yellow Cash Out button carrying a live dollar amount), and the strict color language: green = win/CTA, red = loss, yellow = actionable money.

**FanDuel** is the discipline benchmark: 5 flat slots (Home, All Sports, My Bets, Casino, Account) where the Account tab's label is the live wallet balance ("$42.86"). The bet slip is not a tab; it materializes as a docked bar above the tab bar only when selections exist. Most stealable: balance-as-tab-label (CA analog: live tracked P/L units), "active bets at a glance" pinned on the game page itself so live bettors never context-switch, the Parlay Hub social-proof cards (how many people placed this exact bet, countdown clocks, "next to start" sort), and a signup wizard whose final step delivers value, with the promo restated at the moment of commitment.

**Sleeper** is the identity-first super app: a detached floating pill bar (FANTASY, SCORES, PICKS, FEED, ACCOUNT) with the money product in the accent center slot. Most stealable: username-before-everything onboarding (first input is a fun public handle, phone signup needs no password at all), embedded contextual chat on every surface, the Scores tab badging YOUR players in every live game row, and Super Squads (group chat + shared promo meter + weekly bank) which turns referral into a game. Its floating pill is also the cheapest way for a Capacitor shell to look iOS-26 native.

**Underdog and PrizePicks** are the two-button interaction masters: every player card is a projection number plus Higher/Lower (or More/Less), zero typing. Most stealable: PrizePicks' persistent "Finalize Lineup" banner pinned to the bottom of every browsing surface (counts picks as you tap, opens a bottom-sheet slip); Underdog's slip that slides in on the same screen (reviewers credit it for "fewer taps"); Underdog's Gimme Pick (the first-entry tutorial is a guaranteed-win leg rendered inside the real flow, not an overlay walkthrough); PrizePicks' claim-later rewards that engineer a second session; and full payout-table transparency before commitment. Anti-lesson: PrizePicks' 2025 redesign was slammed specifically for hiding the in-progress slip behind scrolling.

**Pikkit and Betstamp** define state-of-the-art bet tracking. Pikkit: sync-only ledger whose onboarding ends with "N bets imported" and a fully populated dashboard; signature screens are the Units/$/ROI% three-way toggle, the color-coded month P/L calendar with tap-a-day drilldown, and a three-view CLV surface (aggregate % beating close, expected profit over time, ranked where-you-beat-it list). Betstamp: bet entry happens AT the odds screen (tap the market cell, type a stake, confirm) and honest entries earn a blue verified badge because the line actually existed at track time. Most stealable for CA: the verified-at-listed-price badge (nearly free given the T-60 lock), the calendar, the CLV views (CA already has all the data), and Betstamp's Analysis-behind-a-button pattern (deep analytics one tap off My Bets, never a tab slot). Anti-lessons: Pikkit has no manual entry (2+ years of complaints) and paywalled users' own past calendar months (its worst-reviewed decision).

## C. Onboarding and Sign-Up Encouragement Patterns

The category consensus for informational (non-KYC) apps, in flow order:

1. **Guest browse is table stakes.** AN, theScore, ESPN, B/R, Pikkit, Betstamp all let you use the product logged out. Hard walls exist only where regulation forces them (DK/FD/Underdog KYC) or network effects justify them (Sleeper). CA's free #1 pick funnel is the strong version of this; never wall it.
2. **Value carousel: 2-4 slides max, under 60 seconds, visible Skip.** Vevo's A/B: adding Skip lifted logins 10% and registrations 6%.
3. **One taste question only.** ESPN asks favorite teams first run (skippable); theScore defers all favoriting to in-app. Best practice: ask favorite sports, defer teams/books/cappers to first contextual touch.
4. **Demo-the-value empty state, not a feature list.** AN's logged-out My Action: swipeable sample analytics cards, centered "All your bets in one place.", one green "Get started!" button. Precedents outside sports: Copilot Money demo mode, Notion pre-filled workspace, Grammarly demo doc.
5. **Auth sheet skeleton (AN, verified):** headline "Get the tools to make smarter bets", email field + "Sign Up with Email", divider, Google / Apple buttons, "Already a member? Log in", ToS + Privacy footer, low-key "Skip" top-right. The sheet slides over blurred live content so the product is visibly behind it.
6. **Signed-out nag = one collapsible banner, never a modal.** AN's green "Welcome - Start by creating an account" bar docked above the tab bar, riding across tabs, expanding to the auth sheet.
7. **Sign-up triggers at the point of value.** AN: first tracked bet. theScore: chat/favorites sync. Pikkit/Betstamp: first save. Sell the account as continuity ("your bets and votes save to this phone, create a free account to keep them"), ESPN's sync framing.
8. **Notification ask: primer before the OS prompt, after a value moment.** Primer lifts opt-in 2-3x (iOS baseline ~51-56%). ESPN attaches it to team picking; theScore to per-favorite bells; the canonical trigger for CA is right after the first tracked bet or vote ("Get an alert the second this grades"), with a re-prime after the first graded win.
9. **Paywall last, trial inside the button.** RevenueCat 2025: 80% of trial starts happen day one, so one trial screen ends onboarding: annual pre-selected with a save pill, "Start 3-day free trial, then $4/week" inside the button, "No payment due today" line, "Continue with free" escape. Never an interstitial during browsing; AN and ESPN both keep premium as a visible locked destination instead.
10. **Identity tricks worth borrowing:** Sleeper's username-first screen ("pick your handle", fun placeholders) makes signup feel like identity creation, not registration; Underdog's Gimme Pick teaches the flow with a guaranteed win inside the real UI; PrizePicks' claim-later reward forces a second session.
11. **Compliance is lightweight for info apps:** 18+ self-attestation folded into the auth sheet, 1-800-GAMBLER microcopy, "we take no wagers, information only" in the footer. No studied native app shows a cookie banner; consent folds into terms acceptance at signup.

## D. Anatomy of a Best-in-Class Bet History Screen

Synthesized from AN (screenshots 5 and 6), Pikkit, Betstamp, and DK/FD My Bets. Six layers, top to bottom:

**Layer 1: Summary header card.** "Last 30 Days" with net $ (or units), Wins %, ROI %, Record, and a sparkline that renders even flat at $0.00 (AN ships the empty chart on purpose; the chrome IS the promise). Global display toggle: Units / Dollars / ROI % (Pikkit), with dollar amounts hidden by default behind an opt-in reveal.

**Layer 2: Horizontally swipeable period cards.** All Time / Today / Yesterday, each repeating the identical 3-stat grammar ($ net, Record, ROI) plus mini-chart. CSS scroll-snap carousel. (AN verified.)

**Layer 3: Per-sport record cards.** Sport icon, record, net P/L per card; a star badge on the best sport (AN puts it on "NFL 1-0-0 +$0.89"). Derived from the graded ledger only, per CA's record-surface rule.

**Layer 4: Favorite Bets panel.** One stacked horizontal distribution bar per league with a single shared legend (Spread / ML / Total / Props / Futures). Self-knowledge, "what you actually bet". (AN verified.)

**Layer 5: The bet list.** Above it: filter pills (Today / 1D / Last Week / 1M / All) plus a summary strip (ROI / Record / Wins). Then fixed-height rows, each: circular result badge (green check / red X) OVERLAPPING the team logo, bold bet-notation title ("TEX -142", "Over 9.5 (Live) -142", "CWS (F5) -117", period and live markets tagged inline), gray subline "BAL 1 - 7 TEX - Final 10/10/23", right-aligned colored P/L ("+$10.56" green, "-$15.00" red). Segment Open vs Settled at the top (theScore, FD, DK) and offer a filter-and-sort sheet (sport, bet type, date range; DK treats this as first-class). Open-bet cards get the DK anatomy: status chip, per-leg rail for parlays, live score strip, stake / To Win row, and ONE bright action button (DK's yellow Cash Out; CA's analog is a yellow "Live view" deep link to /mylive or the game's live tracker).

**Layer 6: The intelligence layer (one tap deep, never its own tab).** Betstamp puts Analysis behind a top-right button on My Bets; Pikkit drills in from Summary. Contents: the color-coded month P/L calendar (month total pinned, green/red days, tap-a-day drilldown; Pikkit's most-loved screen), breakdowns by sport / bet type / book / odds range / tag, and CLV three ways (aggregate % of bets beating the close, expected profit over time, ranked list of where you beat it). CA can ship CLV honestly and free: T-60 locked lines (ca_line.js) + line_history openers + book_lines_closing already contain everything, and unlike Pikkit (a documented gripe), explain the math on a help page. Stamp manual entries "tracked at listed price" when the line matched a live book row, Betstamp's blue-verify move.

Two guardrails from the leaders' failures: the user's own full history stays free forever (Pikkit's paywalling of past months is its worst-reviewed decision), and open bets stay visible above the fold (PrizePicks' loudest 2025 complaint).

## E. Prioritized Recommendations Mapped to Jack's Five Asks

**P0, Ask 1: Status bar collision.** Every one of the 11 surveyed apps respects the safe area; content scrolls UNDER a translucent header, never into the clock. Fix: `viewport-fit=cover` on the meta viewport, pad the fixed header with `env(safe-area-inset-top)` and the tab bar with `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, and give both a translucent blur fill so content scrolling behind them reads as native (ESPN, Sleeper). Proof: universal; Sleeper's floating pill is the showcase.

**P0, Ask 5: The tab bar.** Ship exactly 5 slots, permanently visible, never hidden on scroll: **Home | Sports | Rankings | Socials | My CA**. Rankings gets the accent treatment (brand green icon + label) as the money slot, at normal height, no raised center button (zero precedent among 11 apps: AN accents PRO, Sleeper accents PICKS, Underdog accents Track). My CA (rightmost) merges My Tracking + Account, AN's My Action pattern: gear inside for settings, pencil for profile; while logged out it shows a person icon opening the auth sheet. Steal FanDuel's label trick: render the signed-in user's live tracked P/L ("+2.3u" in green) as the My CA label. Style the bar as a detached floating rounded pill with translucent dark fill (Sleeper), the cheapest "not a wrapped website" win available to a Capacitor shell. Badge policy: numeric badge on Socials unread (B/R Alerts) and on the Track FAB only. Proof: FanDuel, Hard Rock, Sleeper, AN, tab-bar survey consensus.

**P0, Ask 4a: Track a Bet everywhere, as a FAB + sheet, not a 6th slot.** No betting app spends a tab slot on bet entry; they float it. Green + FAB bottom-right, layered above the tab bar on Home, Sports, Rankings, game detail, and My CA (AN's verified custom-bet FAB). Tapping any pick row or odds cell opens the existing Track a Bet betslip as a half-height bottom sheet (DK); dismissing it keeps the draft and puts a count badge on the FAB (DK center-tab count, PrizePicks Finalize Lineup banner). The slip animates in on the same screen, never a page navigation (Underdog, the "fewer taps" winner). If Jack insists on 6 slots, the precedent is Underdog's dedicated "Track" tab, not a raised button. Proof: AN, DK, FD, PrizePicks, Underdog.

**P0, Ask 3: Intro walkthrough.** Replace any tour overlay with this exact sequence: (1) 2-3 value slides max with a visible Skip (Vevo: Skip lifted registrations 6%); (2) one question, "Which sports do you follow?", tile grid, skippable, written to user_preferences.favorite_sports, stored locally for guests (ESPN, theScore); (3) land directly on the live board with today's #1 pick visible within 60 seconds of install (FanDuel's time-to-value discipline, Sleeper's 30-second bar); (4) auth sheet only at the first save-type action (vote, track, follow), sliding over blurred live content: value headline ("See how your betting really performs"), email field, Apple + Google, "Already a member? Log in", small Skip top-right, 18+ checkbox + 1-800-GAMBLER microcopy in the footer (AN screenshot 2); (5) notification primer only after the first tracked bet or vote, mock push "Your pick just cashed", re-primed once after the first graded win; (6) one trial paywall screen at the very end, annual pre-selected at "about $1.44/week", "Start 3-day free trial, then $4/week" inside the button, "Continue with free" escape. Teach the product with an Underdog-style Gimme moment: reveal one premium pick in place inside Rankings on first run instead of a tooltip tour. Proof: AN, ESPN, theScore, FanDuel, Underdog, RevenueCat/Pushwoosh data.

**P0, Bet history (the AN-grade ask).** Build Section D verbatim inside My CA: summary header card with flat-at-zero sparkline, snap-scroll period cards, per-sport cards with star badge (sportBadge() assets exist), Favorite Bets stacked bars, filter pills + summary strip, and the AN bet-row grammar with (F5)/(Live) inline tags. Open cards get the DK anatomy with a yellow "Live view" button to /mylive. All P/L through utils.flatUnitReturn (the single-source rule). One tap deeper: Pikkit's month calendar and the CLV three-view, CA's unfair advantage since T-60 locks + closing archive make it free and honest, with the math explained. Keep the user's own history free forever. Proof: AN screenshots 5-6, DK, Pikkit, Betstamp.

**P1, Ask 3b/6: Logged-out My CA empty state.** Clone AN screenshot 1 in CA vocabulary: 4-5 swipeable sample cards with fake data ("MLB 61-38, +12.4u" with equity sparkline from the existing mvp.js chart code; ML/Spread/Total stacked bars; a "Line Value" card teasing beat-the-close counts; "Net Units - 30 Days" area chart; a streaks card), then centered "Every bet you make, tracked and graded.", one subline, one green "Get started" button. Keep real chrome (0-0 record, $0.00) rendered, not hidden. Proof: AN verified, Copilot Money, Notion, Grammarly.

**P1, Ask 2: Cookie consent.** Kill the banner in the app shell. No studied native app shows one; consent folds into the auth sheet's legal line ("By continuing you agree to the Terms, Privacy Policy, and analytics use") with granular controls in Settings. Keep the web banner for browser visits only. Proof: category-wide absence (AN, ESPN, theScore, all sportsbooks).

**P1, Ask 4b: Scrolling feel.** One vertical river per tab (theScore's stated engagement driver), horizontal scroll-snap carousels for period/sport cards (AN's swipe feel), fixed-height list rows with `content-visibility: auto` on long bet lists, skeleton placeholders instead of spinners, and keep each tab's DOM alive so tab switches are instant with no reload flash (Sleeper's native-feel reviews). Momentum scrolling via `-webkit-overflow-scrolling` defaults, no nested scroll traps. Proof: theScore, Sleeper, AN.

**P1: Signed-out nag banner.** One collapsible green banner docked above the tab bar ("Welcome. Create a free account to track your bets." + chevron), riding across tabs, expanding to the auth sheet, never blocking content. Replaces every modal prompt. Proof: AN screenshot 3.

**P2: "My Action" module on Home.** ESPN's MY GAMES pattern: compact two-line rows for every game the user has votes or tracked bets on, red live-state column (liveStateHtml() already renders MLB diamond and football down/distance), per-row bell wired to notify_prefs topics, "Result" pill when graded. The /api/my/live feed is exactly this module's data. Proof: ESPN, Sleeper's your-players Scores rows, FanDuel's active-bets-on-game-page.

**P2: Badge-gate the paywall.** Show picks 2-50 as visible locked rows with a small tier badge; tapping opens the upgrade sheet (single sink: startCheckout). Prompt upgrade once at account creation and via locked content, never repeated interstitials. Proof: ESPN badge-gating, AN's PRO-as-destination.

**P2: Retention layer (post-redesign).** iOS Live Activity for tracked bets (score + status on lock screen; DK ships "Tracking Bets on your Lock Screen", theScore calls it their stickiest feature); a claim-later reward after the first graded bet hung on the existing referral +1 day loop (PrizePicks); a year-in-review shareable recap via og_card.js (AN's 2025 Action Recap, Pikkit's Instant Replay); Sleeper-style squad meter on the referral loop.

**Verify by hand before pixel-level mockups:** AN banner persistence across sessions, AN trial wording (7-day free vs paid promo varies), Sleeper slots 1 and 5 labels, PrizePicks Explore vs Promos slot order.
